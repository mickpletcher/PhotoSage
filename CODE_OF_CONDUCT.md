# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

PhotoSage contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation safe,
respectful, and welcoming regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or technology
choices.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's photos, metadata, location,
  credentials, logs, or private paths.
- Use synthetic or sanitized data in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and release readiness.
- Correct mistakes and de-escalate conflict when asked.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, publication of private information, sustained disruption,
and retaliation are not tolerated. Using project channels to probe systems or
data without authorization is also prohibited.

## Enforcement

For ordinary project disagreements, contact the maintainer through the relevant
issue or pull request. For private conduct concerns, use a private contact method
listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile or GitHub's report-abuse channel. Do
not use a public issue when a report identifies a target or includes private
information.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, temporarily limit
participation, or permanently ban a participant when necessary to protect the
project and its community. Reports will be handled as privately and promptly as
practical, with conflicts of interest avoided where possible.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents
PhotoSage in public or private project communication.
